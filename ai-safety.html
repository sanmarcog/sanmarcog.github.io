AI Safety

I use algebraic group actions to study the computations transformers learn for prediction.

Belief states connect the statistical problem to the model's internal representations: they are sufficient for prediction, and representations of them have been observed in transformer residual streams. My work asks how transformers implement belief updating, and which features of the process make a shallow computation possible.

I developed a theory that decomposes belief updating into interacting actions of algebraic groups of matrices. For processes with solvable stabilizer groups, this gives transformer constructions whose depth does not grow with sequence length.

The decomposition also suggests operations to look for in trained models.
