Privacy

I use Umami to see which pages people read, which websites send visitors here, and roughly which countries and cities visitors come from. These statistics are kept for six months.

Analytics uses no cookies, names, email addresses, or custom visitor IDs. This site does not track clicks, searches, or form entries, or make session recordings. Page addresses are sent without query parameters or fragments; referrals include only the referring website.

Umami processes your IP address and browser information to estimate location and group visits using a rotating identifier. IP addresses are not stored in its standard analytics records, although service and security logs may contain them. See Umami’s privacy policy.

Analytics is disabled when your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control. You can also turn it off below. This stores only your choice in this browser; it does not identify you. GitHub hosts this site and handles requests under its privacy statement.
