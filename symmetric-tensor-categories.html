Graduate course

Symmetric Tensor Categories

University of Washington · Winter 2025

Complete lecture notes PDF

From affine group schemes and their representations to Tannakian reconstruction, Deligne’s theorem, semisimplification, and symmetric tensor categories in positive characteristic.

References: Introduction to Affine Group Schemes, by William C. Waterhouse; Tensor Categories, by Pavel Etingof, Shlomo Gelaki, Dmitri Nikshych, and Victor Ostrik. Further references appear in the notes.
