About Me

I am an Argentinian mathematician working on AI safety and mechanistic interpretability.

My background is in representation theory, Hopf algebras, and tensor categories. I am now an Iliad Fellow in London, studying the algebraic structure of belief updating and the computations transformers learn for sequential prediction.

Before moving to London, I was a postdoctoral researcher at the University of Washington with Julia Pevtsova, and at Iowa State University with Shlomo Gelaki. I completed my PhD at the Universidad Nacional de Córdoba under the supervision of Nicolás Andruskiewitsch, followed by a postdoc there with Iván Angiono.
